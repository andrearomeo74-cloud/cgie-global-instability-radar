# CGIE3-ID-02

## Eligible Relation Discovery Protocol

Version: 1.0 (Draft)

---

# Purpose

This protocol defines how candidate relations are evaluated before
they may become part of the candidate identity network.

The objective is not to maximize prediction accuracy.

The objective is to identify reproducible relational structures
that contribute to the preservation of functional identity.

---

# Scientific Question

Which observable relations remain sufficiently stable,
reproducible and estimable to become eligible components of the
candidate identity?

---

# Fundamental Principle

No relation is assumed to be essential.

Every relation must earn eligibility through evidence.

---

# Candidate Relations

Candidate relations may originate from:

- temporal association;
- spatial organization;
- statistical dependence;
- structural coupling;
- domain-specific mechanisms.

Generation of candidate relations must be deterministic and
reproducible.

---

# Eligibility Evaluation

Each candidate relation is evaluated independently.

The evaluation must remain frozen before execution.

No criterion may be modified after observing results.

---

# Eligibility Criteria

Every relation is evaluated according to:

1. estimability;
2. reproducibility;
3. persistence;
4. robustness;
5. uncertainty;
6. continuity;
7. admissibility.

---

# Possible Outcomes

Every evaluated relation receives exactly one status:

- Essential
- Eligible
- Candidate
- Rejected
- Non-estimable

Statuses must remain reproducible.

---

# Interpretation Boundary

Eligibility does not establish:

- causality;
- indispensability;
- prediction.

Eligibility only states that the observed relation satisfies the
declared protocol.

---

# Validation

The protocol itself must undergo:

- placebo tests;
- ablation studies;
- sensitivity analysis;
- reproducibility analysis;
- equivalence analysis.

---

# Scientific Boundary

Failure to identify eligible relations is a valid scientific
outcome.

The framework must never force eligibility to obtain positive
results.
